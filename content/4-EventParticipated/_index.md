# Events Participated

During the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** program, I had the opportunity to participate in a professional event related to Generative AI and AI Agents on AWS.

The event allowed me to explore new technologies beyond the Serverless architecture used in the CloudMenu project, particularly topics related to Amazon Bedrock AgentCore, Memory, Evaluations, Observability, Policy, and Optimization.

---

### [Event 1 – AgentForge Day 2](4.1-Event1/)

&emsp;**Event Name:** AgentForge Day 2 – Advanced Amazon Bedrock AgentCore

&emsp;**Topic:** Personalization, Evaluation & Optimization

&emsp;**Time:** 09:00 – 11:00, August 8, 2026

&emsp;**Role:** Participant

&emsp;**Main Content:** The event focused on advanced capabilities of Amazon Bedrock AgentCore, including Memory, Evaluations, Observability, and AgentCore Components such as Registry, Harness, Tools, Payments, Optimization, and Policy. The event also included a Hands-on Lab that allowed participants to explore several AgentCore capabilities through practical activities.

&emsp;**Key Outcomes:** Gained a better understanding of how AI Agents can be developed, monitored, evaluated, and optimized on AWS. The event also provided foundational knowledge of Generative AI and AI Agent technologies that can support further learning and future applications in cloud-based systems.
