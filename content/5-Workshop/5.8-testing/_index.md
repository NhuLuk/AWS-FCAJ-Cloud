## 5.8. System Testing

After deploying the main components of CloudMenu, the system was tested to verify that the frontend, APIs, and database could operate together successfully.

The testing process focused on two main areas:

- **API Testing:** verifying the order creation, order retrieval, and order status update APIs.
- **Functional Testing:** verifying the actual CloudMenu interfaces and workflows for customers and restaurant staff.
